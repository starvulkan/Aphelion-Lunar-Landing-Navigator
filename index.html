    why it is hard

    At the Moon's south pole the Sun skims the horizon, never more than a degree or two up. One ridge gets months of light. The crater beside it has been dark for billions of years. Earth bobs up and down twice a month, and with it your radio link. Pick the right place and the right day, and a lander lives.
